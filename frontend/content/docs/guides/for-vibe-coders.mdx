## 1. Create an account

1. Open your Kova instance in the browser (hosted default: `https://kova-9269c6.rumptycloud.app`).
2. Go to [Sign up](/auth/signup).
3. Register with email and password, then sign in at [Log in](/auth/login).

## 2. Complete onboarding

After sign-in, open [Onboarding](/onboarding). The wizard has four steps:

| Step | What you do | What Kova does |
|---|---|---|
| Project | Project name + **base URL** of the app under test | Creates the project |
| Credentials | Optional login username/password for the app | Stores credentials for later runs |
| Discovery | Start exploration | Opens an isolated browser and discovers workflows |
| First validation | Kick off a first check | Runs an initial validation and shows results |

You can re-run onboarding later; it is not a one-time gate.

## 3. Explore manually

Open [Explore](/explore?url=https://your-app.example) to drive a live session against any URL. Useful for checking login, discovering pages, and seeing how Kova reads your UI.

## 5. Read results

Open a completed run to see status, step-by-step timeline, screenshots, and verification details explaining **why** it passed or failed.

See [Results and evidence](results-and-evidence) for how to interpret verdicts.

## 7. Next level (optional)

When you are ready to automate:

- Create an [API token](/settings/tokens)
- Point CI at the CI endpoints or `scripts/kova_ci.py` — see [CI/CD](../integrations/ci-cd)
- Connect GitHub/GitLab under project **Integrations** — see [Integrations overview](../integrations/overview)

> [!NOTE]
> Prefer a **staging or preview URL** until you trust the flows. Short, clear paths beat giant journeys. Evidence (screenshots + timeline) is the source of truth.
